Coco themes: six themes, light and dark

Every swatch and every miniature below is painted from the generated colour tables the app itself reads (the same variables, the same panel rules), so this is what the app will look like. Theme 1 is the founders' own and is locked; themes 2 to 6 are generated from their three seed colours with Google's Material Color Utilities.

Contrast figures are WCAG ratios of the exact colours shown. The build refuses any theme whose text pairs fall below 4.5:1. Typeface: Space Grotesk (SIL Open Font License).
