## Standing rules that come from taste
1. Never a flat recolor: every kit ships real motion (looping signature animation, springy or glide entrances, press feedback).
2. No yellow anywhere in a kit (also avoid lime and amber that read as yellow). Warning roles are left out on purpose.
3. No dashes in product, file or kit names. Plain words or underscores.
4. Free fonts only, licence written down, bundled locally (no runtime Google calls in extensions).
5. Each product gets its own colours, type and motion; same Material You foundation (full M3 roles, shape scale, state layers).
6. Prove every kit by rendering it and looking at the screenshot before handover.
