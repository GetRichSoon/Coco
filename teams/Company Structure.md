Decided by Mangesh (Boss), 2026-10-15. Coco keeps this current.

## The company
```
GOOD SPACE  (owners: Mangesh "Boss", Shreya "Big Boss")
COCO ............ the manager
HOUSES .......... what we make
  Gigi .......... content (videos)            working
  Lulu .......... products (extensions, apps) working
  Fifi .......... downloads (notes, templates) next
  Zaza .......... games                        later
PLATFORMS ....... where we sell, shared by every House
  YouTube, Play Store, Chrome Web Store, Roblox, Website, Instagram/Facebook
BONBON CLUB ..... helpers for every House
  Lola ideas, Lili research + results, Remy builds, Margot writes, Cleo videos,
  Elise checks quality, Kiki growth, Didi customer care, Bibi money + reports
SHARED SHELF .... everything we make, reused by all Houses (80% own work, 20% helping others)
```
Bonbon Club names map to the agent files in Coco HQ/.claude/agents: Lola+Lili = scout, Remy = builder,
Margot = content-writer, Cleo = video-producer, Elise = release-qa, Kiki = growth; Didi and Bibi are new roles.

Flow: idea → make → check → founder YES → at least 3 platforms → track money.
Rules: 3+ platforms per idea · free leads to paid · grow / fix / pause by profit · only 2-3 Houses
active · nothing public, no spending, no deleting without a YES.

Every House uses one shape: **House → Platform → Category → Channel / Product**.
