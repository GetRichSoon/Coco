# Bonbon Club Toolkit

Every tool belongs to the support team (Bonbon Club). The Houses decide what to make and judge the
results; the Bonbon Club does the work with these tools for all four Houses. Founder decision 2026-10-15.

Rule: free tools and free plans only. Nothing paid without a founder YES. Every product keeps a
Licence Log of the fonts, icons, photos and assets it uses; Elise checks it before release.

Status: **connected** = Coco can use it directly · **installed** = on the PC · **web** = opens in the
browser · **to install** = free, not installed yet · **extension** = free browser extension.
