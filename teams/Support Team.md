# 🤝 Support Team

Six AI members shared by every main team (founder decision, 2026-10-14). Main teams own the
goal; the Support Team does the hands-on work for them. Coco calls each member in order and
passes the work along. QA is always a different member from the one who built the work.

| Member | Job | Helps |
|---|---|---|
| Scout | Finds and scores ideas, trends and topics | YouTube Studio, Extension Atelier, App Garden, Social Lounge |
| Builder | Designs and builds extensions, apps and websites | Extension Atelier, App Garden |
| Release QA | Tests the work and packs the release | all four |
| Content Writer | Scripts, captions, titles, store listing text | all four |
| Video Producer | Turns scripts into finished videos | YouTube Studio, Social Lounge |
| Growth | Marketing plans, replies and outreach (drafts only) | all four |

Usual flows:
- **Extension or app:** Scout → Builder → Release QA → Content Writer (listing) → founder YES.
- **Video:** Scout → Content Writer → Video Producer → Release QA → post (Shorts) or founder YES (long).

Their full instructions live in `Coco HQ/.claude/agents/`.
