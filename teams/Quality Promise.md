# The Quality Promise

Everything Good Space gives to a user is a good, high-quality product. Founder rule, 2026-10-15.
This applies to every video, extension, app, website, download, game, store page, reply and post.

## Who may make what users see
- Final work for users is made with Claude (Sonnet, or Opus for hard work) by the right Bonbon Club member.
- Local AI (Ollama) only prepares rough, internal work. Its output never reaches a user as is.
- Saving tokens never lowers quality: if a cheap model's result is not good enough, the work moves up a level.

## Elise's gate (nothing goes out without it)
1. **It works**: tested for real (on the device, in the browser, in the store preview), not just read.
2. **It looks like Good Space**: follows the Design Atelier, light and dark, readable contrast.
3. **It is correct**: facts checked, numbers checked, no spelling mistakes, honest claims only.
4. **It is ours**: original, Licence Log complete, no copied screens, art, music or brands.
5. **It is useful**: solves the problem it promises, quickly and simply.
6. **It follows platform rules**: YouTube, Chrome Web Store, Play Store, Roblox.
7. **Founder YES** before anything public, paid or deleted.

A FAIL from Elise sends the work back to the maker with the exact fix; it is checked again after the fix.

## After launch
Lili tracks results, Didi reads every review. Real complaints become fixes in the next version.
