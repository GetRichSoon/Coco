Every The Flow video, Shorts and long videos, follows the animation theme of the latest 2 Shorts (pillow-charging and contagious-yawn): **habit-beats**. That is flat vector art drawn with cairo, the hero Kai, one bold full-screen colour per beat, big short titles, props that pop with bounce, at 60 fps. The renderers are in studio/faceless/pillow-charging/ and studio/faceless/yawn-contagious/. Long videos use it at 16:9.

Boss also named a "more like this" reference on 2026-10-14: a Kurzgesagt-style habits explainer, saved on his PC as Downloads/WhatsApp Video 2026-10-07 at 4.37.20 PM.mp4. Its frame sheet is company/youtube/refs/habits-explainer-style-sheet.jpg. Take style only: full illustrated scenes with depth and light, a hero plus animal sidekick (ours: Kai and Orbi), one colour mood per scene, icon cards and banner titles. Never copy their characters or art.

Template for every new video, saved 2026-10-14: studio/the-flow/habit/TEMPLATE.md with hb.py and style_ref.py. It has the THEME palette, camera push, dust, light, sparkles, Orbi lip-sync and the Kokoro af_heart voice. Reference clips are in content/2026-10-14-style-reference/.

**Why:** Boss, 2026-10-14, right after long video 01 was published in a different style (Volt mascot on a starfield, studio/the-flow/long). That style is not the house style.

**How to apply:** Before building any Short or long video, copy the habit-beats pattern. Do not reuse the Volt long-video look. Make the opening beats as lively as the later ones. Rule is saved in company/youtube/QUALITY_V2.md and company/DECISIONS.md. Related: [[project-youtube-the-flow]]
