The Velvet Foundry = premium international publishing brand, but the goal is commercially competitive books that buyers find, buy, read and recommend, not luxury-looking covers.

**Why:** Boss (2026-11-07) rejected "every book black, gold and luxury". Luxury must raise perceived quality and trust, never reduce clarity or commercial appeal.

**How to apply:**
- Nine books: the ORIGINAL FOUR first (ASWB 90-day plan, Keep Your Own Books, The Retake Method, Where Your AWS Bill Goes; EPUB-only, live at thevelvetfoundry.gumroad.com, 3 of 4 in category "Other"), then the five new ones (AI Workflow Playbook, Client Acquisition Playbook, Python Automation for Beginners, Practical Money System, Job Interview Playbook; live 2026-11-07).
- Each book follows its OWN category's visual conventions and buyer expectations; keep the publisher identity in typography principles, interior formatting, chapter openers, diagram quality, editorial quality.
- Per book: market positioning (customer, problem, willingness to pay, competitors offer/fail, why ours is better), 10+ competitors (price, ratings, cover, content depth, search visibility), content check (completeness, depth, accuracy, templates, references, editing).
- Weak products: prepare 2-3 cover concepts and compare on category recognition, title readability, thumbnail visibility, buyer relevance, brand fit (not just taste).
- Never change live artwork, files, prices or URLs without Boss approval; never invent sales or call a book a bestseller.
