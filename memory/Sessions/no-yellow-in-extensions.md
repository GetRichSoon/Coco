Mangesh (2026-10-23): "don't use that yellow color in extension, use any other".
Avoid `--mu-c-marigold` and `--mu-c-butter` (and `mu-duo-*butter*`, `mu-duo-wine-marigold`) in extension popups, pages and logos.
Use sky, bubblegum, mauve, tomato, cherry, cobalt, wine, plum, forest, cream instead.

**Why:** He dislikes how the yellow looks in the extensions.
**How to apply:** Every extension redesign and logo. See [[material-you-theme]], [[material-you-motion-required]].
