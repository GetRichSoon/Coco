For every piece of company work (video topics, extension ideas, app ideas, course topics, game ideas), research with real tools before deciding: Google Trends (trends.google.com, rising queries), YouTube search autocomplete (suggestqueries.google.com ds=yt), view counts of the top channels in the niche (yt-dlp), store charts and similar sites. Write the numbers and sources into the decision file.

**Why:** Boss, 2026-10-25: "use google trend like website tools etc for all what u need for any company work". He wants data-backed picks, e.g. aiming the next Flow Short at 1M views.

**How to apply:** start every topic or idea pick with a quick data pass (trend + search demand + what already wins in the niche), then choose. Related: [[feedback-reference-quality]], [[feedback-do-more-than-asked]].
