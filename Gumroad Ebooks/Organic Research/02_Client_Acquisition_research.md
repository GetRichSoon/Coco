## Observations

- Price spread: free to $118. Median of paid USD-ish listings is about $19.99 to $29. Only the one proven page (601 sales shown) sits at $118 and it is a course, not an ebook.
- Strong-signal items are few: grflix (601 sales shown, 32 ratings), clientbytes (15 ratings), flickman (26 sales).
- Most listings are narrow: templates only, cold email only, beginners only, writers only, or AI-flavoured. Few cover the full chain lead sourcing, outreach, booked call, discovery call, proposal, close.

## Title-structure patterns

1. "Cold Email/Outreach Playbook" plus a number ("40+ Templates", "50 Templates"): template-count titles are the most common.
2. "How to Get Your First Client / High-Paying Clients": beginner promise, outcome-first.
3. Time-boxed outcome ("in 30 Days", "in Under A Month", "in 7 Days").
4. Audience tag in title: "for Freelancers", "Dev Agency", "Writing Client".
5. "Playbook", "Blueprint", "Guide", "Mastery" as the noun; "Playbook" already used by several, so it is not distinctive on its own.
6. Rare: the whole pipeline in one title (lead, call, proposal, win). This is the gap.

## Price recommendation

Recommend $19 to $24 (suggest $19). Reasoning from table: the broad ebook field clusters $5 to $30 with almost no proof of demand; $39 to $57 pages show 0 to 3 ratings; $118 works only as a course with scripts. A complete system with checklists and scripts can credibly sit above $10 template packs and at or below $29. Use pay-what-you-want minimum or a clear launch price only if it is honest and stated plainly; no fake "was" prices, no fake reviews, no paid ads or spam. Revisit after real purchase and review data.
