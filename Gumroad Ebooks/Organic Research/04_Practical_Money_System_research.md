## 2. Title-structure analysis
- Common patterns: "[Outcome] Guide/Blueprint/Plan" (Budgeting Blueprint, Your Money Plan, Debt Payoff Plan); "Beginner's ..." (Beginner's Guide to Financial Freedom, Beginner's Budgeting Template); "[Topic] + [Topic]" (Budget Binder + Money Saving Guide); time-boxed (30 Days, 30-Day Financial Transformation); audience-tagged (in Your 20s).
- Heavy use of "Financial Freedom", "Blueprint", "Formula" (Zero-Sum/Failproof Budgeting Formula); these are generic and crowded.
- Few titles state the full scope (budget, save, debt, invest) in the title itself; subtitle space is under-used for plain-language search phrases.
- Many titles use "Planner/Template/Tracker/Workbook"; buyers searching for a book/guide explanation are less served.

## 3. Underserved long-tail phrases (hypotheses, volumes NOT verified)
- "budgeting for beginners step by step" - few listings pair a step-by-step system with all four pillars.
- "how to pay off debt and start saving at the same time" - debt listings are debt-only.
- "budget saving debt investing in one guide" - only the free Beginner's Guide overlaps.
- "personal finance basics for beginners pdf" - generic but few PDF-guide titles say "basics".
- "money system for beginners" / "simple money system" - "system" framing is rare versus "blueprint".
- "emergency fund how much beginners" - not surfaced as a title anywhere.
- "debt snowball vs avalanche explained" - mentioned in some descriptions, rarely a title.
- "personal finance guide any country / international" - listings mix EUR/USD/PHP; no clear "works in any currency" positioning.
- "first budget after paying off debt: what next" - gap between debt and wealth steps.

## 5. Price recommendation
Suggest 9 USD list price (pay-what-you-want not recommended). Reasoning: median of visible paid listings is about 7 to 10 USD; comparable bundles with worksheets sit at 15 to 17 USD with 3 to 5 shown sales; free and 1 to 5 USD guides show few proof signals; a full guide covering four pillars justifies upper-middle pricing without needing proof. With zero reviews at launch, 9 USD stays below the 10 to 17 band where worksheet bundles sit. Revisit after real reviews arrive (no fake reviews, no misleading "was/now" discounts; if an intro price is used, label it honestly as an intro price with an end date).

## 6. Draft SEO copy
- Title: The Practical Money System: A Beginner's Guide to Budgeting, Saving, Debt Payoff & Building Wealth
- Subtitle: A step-by-step plan to budget, build an emergency fund, pay down debt and start investing, in any currency.
- First paragraph: Most money guides teach one skill and leave you guessing about the rest. The Practical Money System puts budgeting, saving, debt payoff and beginner investing into one simple order of steps you can follow at your own pace. This is general education, not personal financial advice, and it makes no promises about results.

## 7. Income / wealth guarantees in these listings
Scan was of titles and the description snippets visible only; not a full read of every description. Examples with outcome-leaning language: "Failproof Budgeting Formula" (gumroad.com/l/thefailproofbudgetingformula, title only, not extracted), "Financial Freedom" titles, "Destroy Your Debt", "30 Day Financial Transformation" (result outcome language in snippet), "Grow Wealth Daily / Wealth Builder". No explicit dollar-amount income guarantee was seen in the extracted snippets; wording is aspirational. Avoid "failproof", "guaranteed", "financial freedom in N days" in our listing.

## 8. Gumroad prohibited page (checked via curl on 2026-11-07, https://gumroad.com/prohibited)
- The prohibited list includes "get rich quick" schemes, business and investment opportunities, mortgage consulting, credit repair, and security brokerage services; it also lists deceptive marketing practices and crypto/NFT investments.
- Another section says a product that guarantees an outcome, or substitutes for a professional the buyer should see, is prohibited (stated in the fortune-telling/readings entry, but the principle is the same test: what the product promises).
- Applied to us: educational budgeting/debt/investing explanation is fine; do not promise returns or income, do not sell investment picks or credit repair, and state clearly it is education, not advice. (Summary from page text; read the page itself before publishing.)
