## 2. Title-structure analysis

- Most common pattern: "[Adjective] Interview [Playbook/eGuide/Guide]" with no number or audience (rows 2, 3, 5). Generic, hard to rank, no keyword beyond "interview".
- Number-led titles ("50 Tough...", "55 Unconventional...", "24 Questions to Ask") are the clearest question-led pattern. Our "100 Common Interview Questions" is the largest count seen on a general-audience Gumroad title, and "Common" matches how people phrase the search.
- "Playbook" is used by at least 3 competitors (chiatiibim "Ultimate Interview Playbook" at 10 EUR, bpmangus "Ultimate Job Search Playbook", Nail the Interview style). Direct name clash risk: "The Ultimate Interview Playbook" is nearly our title. Mitigation: keep "Job Interview" and the number in the title, and lead the SEO title with the question count and "answers".
- Bundles that stack resume + LinkedIn + interview exist only in generic or AI-prompt framing (row 11, 22). None found that combines 100 Q&A with named answer frameworks plus resume/LinkedIn for graduates.
- Specific-audience titles appear mostly for niches (product managers, freelancers, tech). No "graduate / first job / career changer" interview title was found in these searches.
- Colons and parenthetical keyword stacking (row 11) are common; they hurt readability. Better: one clear title, keyword-rich subtitle.

## 3. Underserved long-tail phrases (hypotheses, volumes NOT verified)

Each is justified by a gap seen above. Test by checking Gumroad search suggestions and Google autocomplete before committing.

1. "interview questions and answers for freshers / graduates" : no graduate-specific general title seen.
2. "first job interview questions and answers" : same gap; competitors target "ambitious job seekers" or niches.
3. "career change interview answers" / "why are you changing careers answer" : not addressed by any title seen.
4. "STAR method examples interview answers" : STAR search returned no Gumroad products at all, only blogs; Gumroad supply for STAR looks absent.
5. "100 common interview questions and answers pdf" : largest competing count seen is 55; 100 is open.
6. "tell me about yourself answer examples" : classic question; competitors do not name individual questions in titles.
7. "interview answer framework" : few titles name a framework; ours bundles several.
8. "resume and LinkedIn tips for graduates / no experience" : existing resume/LinkedIn guides are general or role-niche (PM, freelancers).
9. "interview questions to ask the employer" : only a free 10-page item seen; could be a chapter and a tag.
10. "behavioral interview questions and answers pdf" : same STAR gap.

## 5. Price recommendation

Observed prices for comparable general interview ebooks: $4.99 to $10 (rows 1 to 4), outlier $25. Resume/LinkedIn guides: $7.99 to $20. The only seller-shown sales counts sit at $20 (34) and $39 (3), but that is two data points, not a trend, and ratings are not sales.

Recommendation: list at $12.99 and keep it there. Reasoning: it is above the cheap 100-question competitor (10 EUR) because it also covers resume + LinkedIn + frameworks (three products' worth), yet below the $15 to $20 bundle band where a new seller with no reviews carries extra risk. Do not run a fake "was $29" discount. Honest levers instead: a real, clearly labeled launch price only if it truly ends, or a $0+ minimum on a short free sample chapter (cf. row 6) to collect first ratings. Revisit after 10 real sales and any genuine reviews.
