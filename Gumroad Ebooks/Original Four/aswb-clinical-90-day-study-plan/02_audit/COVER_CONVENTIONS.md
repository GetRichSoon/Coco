Covers downloaded to `competitor_covers\` from the public Gumroad page JSON and each one looked at: c01 to c09 (nine files). Kaplan, Springer, Test Prep Books and Etsy covers were not downloaded (not on Gumroad / not reachable), so conclusions come from the nine Gumroad covers only. ## 1. What winning covers in this niche have in common

(Winning here means: the most complete-looking, clearest listings; I have no sales data and ratings were 0 or not visible, so I do not claim any is a bestseller.)

- **The exam name is the loudest thing.** "LCSW", "ASWB", "LCSW & LMSW candidates", "Compatible with August 2026 ASWB exam". A buyer scanning results finds their exam in under a second. Ours says "Clinical Licensing Exam" in small, light-weight lettering and never says LCSW or ASWB on the thumbnail.
- **A year or edition marker** ("2026 Exam Cycle", "2026-2027 Edition", "August 2026"). Format changed in August 2026 so recency is a buying trigger.
- **Numbers as proof of volume**: "200 practice questions", "99 pages", "61 practice questions", "150 question". The reader benefit is shown as a countable thing.
- **Badges, pills and ribbons**: feature pills, price corner ribbon, burst badges.
- **Bright, saturated or high-contrast colour**: magenta, pale blue with navy, lavender with navy, navy with gold. Dark navy with gold appears once (c06) and is made louder by big sans type and badges. Our dark navy with cream serif is the quietest cover in the set.
- **Heavy sans-serif, very large title**, title takes 40 to 60% of the area.
- **Mockup of the product** (page fan, tablet) on c06, c08, c09: shows what is inside. Strongest signal of a real, finished product.
- **Creator credential** (LCSW-SIFI line) on the Ayo covers: trust. We cannot copy this (no licensed author) and must not fake it; use a plain publisher line instead.

## 2. What looks amateur

- c07: a logo-like shape with no title: invisible in search.
- c04/c05: a Canva template with a long all-caps bullet list; reads as a worksheet, not a book.
- c01: large empty middle, faint low-contrast icons, mixed fonts.
- c09: the tablet image dominates; the book title is not on the cover.
- c08: collage with a floral sticker and cursive script; generic school look.
- Generic things to avoid in any cover: stock brain graphics, tiny text lines under 20 pixels at thumbnail size, pass guarantees.

## 3. Our current cover, against the above (live_cover.jpg 1005x565, live_thumbnail.jpg 600x600)

- Good: strong "90-DAY" hero, very legible, calm, premium; the unusual calendar-grid motif is memorable.
- Weak: no exam keyword on the thumbnail; no year; no number beyond "90"; the 4 rows of squares are not self-explanatory; copper and cream tiny lines (publisher, subtitle, disclaimer) are unreadable at 300 pixels; author is "The Velvet Foundry Editorial Team", which tells a buyer nothing; navy and copper is close to the palette of c06 (navy/gold) but quieter, so we look like a lower-effort version of it.
- The disclaimer line on the cover ("Not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by ASWB. Contains no examination questions.") is correct and should stay in the product but is too small to read; put it on the back/first page and in the description, and keep one short line on the cover only if it can be at least 22 px at 1005 px width.

## 4. Brief rules that apply to all three concepts

- Must read at 300 px wide: "LCSW" or "ASWB CLINICAL", "90-DAY", and "STUDY PLAN" must be legible.
- Never use ASWB's logo, colours designed to mimic ASWB, or the words "official", "approved", "endorsed", "guaranteed", "pass". "ASWB" may appear only as a descriptive name of the exam, with an independence line, not as the brand of the book.
- Do not show an exam screen or anything resembling Pearson VUE branding.
- Publisher identity: small "The Velvet Foundry" wordmark and a consistent corner motif (the 13-week grid is the recognisable brand device); but the dominant palette should fit the category rather than be dark navy and gold.
- No yellow/gold dominance and no Opera or AWS imagery (not relevant here); avoid stock brains.
- Deliver: 1600x2560 portrait cover (main), 1005x565 landscape poster, 600x600 square thumbnail, same hierarchy in each.

## 5. Three distinct concepts

### Concept A: "The Wall Calendar" (education-clear, light)
- **Palette**: warm white #fbf8f1 background, ink navy #0f2238 text, teal #1f8f8a for done days, one accent coral #e4572e for exam day. Publisher mark in copper #b87333, small.
- **Layout**: portrait. Top third: pill "LCSW EXAM · 2026 FORMAT". Middle: huge "90-DAY" (about 28% of cover height), under it "Study Plan" in the same weight, then one line: "For the ASWB Clinical exam: retake or first attempt". Bottom half: a real 13-column by 7-row calendar grid (days 1 to 90, with the four phases colour-coded and a coral square on day 90) taking the full width.
- **Headline hierarchy**: 1) 90-DAY STUDY PLAN; 2) LCSW EXAM; 3) subtitle; 4) four phase labels under the grid (Diagnose, Rotate, Consolidate, Taper); 5) small publisher and independence line.
- **Image idea**: the calendar grid is the cover; it is literally the product. The grid on the live cover is a dim version of this idea; here it becomes large, labelled and coloured.
- **Why a buyer recognises it**: it looks like a planner (c08/c09 conventions) but with the exam name loud like c06; bright enough to stand out against navy/gold and pink.

### Concept B: "Day 90 Countdown" (urgency, bold sans, high contrast)
- **Palette**: electric teal #0e9aa7 background, white and near-black #0b1620 type, a single orange #ff7a1a countdown number. Publisher mark white.
- **Layout**: portrait. Giant stacked numerals "90" in orange, dominating the upper half; to its right a vertical ribbon "LCSW". Below: "DAY STUDY PLAN" in heavy white sans. Lower third: three icon-bullets on a dark panel ("Dated for every day", "Spaced review", "Pacing rehearsal for 122 questions"), then a pill "2026 EXAM FORMAT".
- **Headline hierarchy**: 1) 90 (huge number); 2) DAY STUDY PLAN; 3) LCSW / ASWB CLINICAL; 4) the three benefits; 5) pill.
- **Image idea**: a stylised countdown: the numeral 90 filled with a faint grid of 90 small squares, the last one outlined. No people, no brains.
- **Why a buyer recognises it**: same loud sans-and-badge grammar as the Ayo and Agents of Change covers, so it reads as exam-prep in a glance; the number is the benefit, as "200 questions" is on c06.

### Concept C: "The Study Binder" (printable, tool-first, mockup)
- **Palette**: soft sage #dfe9e2 background, deep green #1d4a3f type, cream pages #fffdf7, one copper #b87333 tab-and-clip accent (keeps Velvet Foundry identity without navy).
- **Layout**: square-friendly (also works portrait). Left or top: title block "90-DAY STUDY PLAN for the LCSW (ASWB Clinical) exam" in heavy green sans with a copper underline. Centre/right: a three-ring binder or fanned printable pages mock-up showing real pages from the book (the phase table, the review grid diagram, the scorecard), each with a legible title ("Weekly scorecard", "Review schedule", "Pacing checkpoints"). Strip at the bottom with three counted claims that are true of the product (for example "5 diagrams", "13-week calendar", "printable scorecards": only once those exist in the final product).
- **Headline hierarchy**: 1) title; 2) audience line "For retakers and first attempts"; 3) binder mockup with page captions; 4) true feature strip; 5) publisher mark.
- **Image idea**: product mockup of the actual inside pages (the same device c06 uses). The diagrams created in `03_improved\diagrams\` can be used as the pages.
- **Why a buyer recognises it**: tool-and-template look is how planners are sold on Gumroad (c08, c09); the exam name and year make it exam-specific, which those two lack.

## 6. Notes for the designer
- All claims on the cover must match the shipped product (page count, "printable", "PDF"); do not add numbers that are not true.
- Do not imply a pass result. No "pass", "guaranteed", "ace the exam".
- If the owner adds a PDF edition, put "PDF + EPUB" on the cover strip.
- The thumbnail (600x600) is the make-or-break asset: test at 150 px; if "LCSW" and "90" are not instantly legible, enlarge them.
