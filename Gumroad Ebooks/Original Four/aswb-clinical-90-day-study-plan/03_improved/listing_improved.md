## Description (paste as plain text)

A 90-DAY PLAN FOR THE ASWB CLINICAL (LCSW) EXAM, DAY BY DAY

You can download ASWB's free content outline and a blank study plan template. What is missing is the decision about what you study on day 14, and how you know by day 40 whether it is working. This book is that decision, written out for 90 days.

It is written first for candidates waiting out the 90-day gap before a retake. The method works for a first attempt too, and the book says which parts to adjust.

WHAT YOU GET
• A four-phase calendar: diagnose (days 1 to 7), rotate (days 8 to 63), consolidate (days 64 to 84), taper (days 85 to 90)
• A weekly shape you can repeat: five study days, one review day, one off day, plus one slot kept free for catching up
• Study time allocated by the published 2026 content-area weights (Values and Ethics 36%, Assessment and Planning 32%, Intervention and Practice 32%), with a worked example you can copy
• A 90-minute study-block method built on retrieval, not re-reading
• How to write your own recall and decision practice questions
• A spaced-review schedule on named days
• Pacing practice for 122 questions in four hours, with checkpoints
• A diagnostic week, a weekly scorecard and three rules for changing the plan
• What to cut, and what never to cut, when you fall behind, plus a compressed plan
• How to read a score report without over-reading it
• The re-registration, waiting-period and waiver steps as ASWB published them, in the order to do them
• An exam-day and day-before guide
ONLY IF the tools appendix ships: • Blank calendar, scorecard and logistics pages to copy
ONLY IF the diagram edition ships: • Five diagrams: the phases, the study block, the review schedule, the pacing checkpoints and the "what to do when behind" decision flow

WHO IT IS FOR
• Licensed-track social workers preparing for the ASWB Clinical exam on the 2026 format
• Retakers who want a structure for the 90-day wait
• Anyone who has plenty of content and no schedule

WHO IT IS NOT FOR
• Anyone looking for practice questions, clinical teaching or exam content. This book has none
• Anyone expecting a guaranteed result. Preparation helps; the outcome depends on you and on the day

HOW MUCH TIME IT ASSUMES
About ten to twelve hours a week for 90 days. The book explains how to compress the plan if you have less time or a waiver moves your date.

IMPORTANT
This is an independent book from The Velvet Foundry. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, approved by, sponsored by or connected to the Association of Social Work Boards (ASWB), Pearson VUE or any licensing board. ASWB and exam names are used only to say which exam the plan is designed for. It contains no examination questions or confidential material. It does not guarantee any exam result and makes no claim about pass rates. Exam rules, fees and formats change: check ASWB's current published material and your own board. Not clinical, legal or licensure advice.
