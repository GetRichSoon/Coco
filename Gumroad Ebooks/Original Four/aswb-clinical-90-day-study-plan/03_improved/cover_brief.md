# Cover brief for the lead designer (no covers were made here)

Detailed concepts and competitor evidence: `..\02_audit\COVER_CONVENTIONS.md`. This file is the short brief.

## What the book is
An independent 90-day study plan for the ASWB Clinical (LCSW) exam on the 2026 format (122 questions, three content areas). A method and schedule book, EPUB (PDF possible later). It contains no exam questions and no clinical teaching. Publisher: The Velvet Foundry.

## The reader
A social worker with an MSW and supervised hours, usually mid-career, stressed, short of time (ten to twelve hours a week). Most often a retaker inside the 90-day wait; also first-timers. They browse Gumroad or Google on a phone and compare it with pink or navy-gold exam covers that shout "LCSW".

## Strongest single visual idea
**The 90-day calendar as the cover.** A big, clean 13-column by 7-row grid of 90 day-cells (the four phases in four colours, day 90 outlined or marked), because the product literally is a dated calendar. It is what the live cover hints at with its squares, but too small, too dark and unlabelled. Alternative: a "90" numeral built from that grid.

## Title hierarchy
1. Main title (huge, heavy sans): **90-DAY STUDY PLAN**
2. Exam line (second largest, a pill or band): **LCSW · ASWB CLINICAL EXAM · 2026 FORMAT** (descriptive use of the exam name only)
3. Subtitle (readable at 150 px wide on a thumbnail, otherwise omit): "Day-by-day schedule for retakers and first attempts"
4. Small: The Velvet Foundry wordmark; one-line independence note ("Independent. Not affiliated with ASWB.") only if legible at 22 px at 1005 px width, else keep it on page one and in the description.

Do not use the long live title on the cover; use the shorter proposed title from `listing_improved.md`.

## Palette suggestion
Category-fit first, brand second (do not default to dark navy and gold, which is already one competitor's look):
- Concept A: warm white, ink navy, teal, one coral day-90 marker, copper publisher mark.
- Concept B: bright teal with orange numerals and white type.
- Concept C: sage and deep green with copper tab.
Pick one concept; keep copper #b87333 as the small recurring Velvet Foundry accent.

## Things to avoid
- ASWB logo, any ASWB or Pearson VUE branding, anything looking like an exam screen or testing centre, any seal or badge that suggests approval.
- The words official, approved, endorsed, guaranteed, pass, ace, "pass first time".
- Fake credentials, fake reviews, "bestseller", "#1", or numbers that are not true of the shipped file (pages, questions, templates).
- Stock brains, stethoscopes, generic puzzle pieces, tiny text under 20 px at thumbnail size, low-contrast icons (seen on competitor c01).
- Cream serif small caps on navy (current look): too quiet in this category.

## Thumbnail legibility rules
- Square 600x600 must read at 150 px: "90-DAY", "STUDY PLAN" and "LCSW" must be legible; nothing else is required.
- Minimum cap height for the exam pill: 7% of the cover height.
- High contrast: text to background at least 7:1 for the three main phrases.
- Same hierarchy in portrait 1600x2560, landscape 1005x565 poster and square thumbnail; no text that appears in one and not the others except the small line.
- No more than 3 colours plus the grid; no gradients behind text.
