# Diagrams for The Retake Method (5)

All are SVG (1200 px wide, Georgia, navy / cream / copper / teal) with a PNG render checked by eye. Each one only restates what the manuscript already says. Insertion points are in `..\manuscript_with_diagrams.md`.

| File | Placed in | Shows | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01_diagnostic_two_day | Chapter 5, before "When to find a subject tutor" | Day one untimed (reading, knowledge, decision), day two timed (adds pacing, nerves), count the marks, four outcomes with chapter pointers | The single most important decision in the book (what to fix first) becomes one page a reader can pin up |
| 02_reading_routine | Chapter 6, before "A worked example" | The six-step reading routine | A routine is meant to be run identically on every question; a numbered picture is faster to recall than a numbered paragraph |
| 03_four_decision_rules | Chapter 7, before "Putting the rules into practice" | Rule, test question and trap for FIRST, NEXT, BEST, MOST LIKELY, plus the LEAST/EXCEPT/NOT sorting rule | The chapter's core reference, readable at a glance on a phone |
| 04_tiebreaker_sequence | Chapter 8, before the second "A worked example" | The eight tie-breaker lines in order | The book tells the reader to learn these eight lines by heart; this is the thing to learn from |
| 05_pacing_checkpoints | Chapter 10, before "What to do at a checkpoint" | The 100 questions / 150 minutes example: 84 seconds each, checkpoints at Q25/50/75/100, 10 minute buffer, countdown equivalents, skip rule | Arithmetic and timeline are easier to follow drawn than described; numbers match the text exactly |

Not diagrammed on purpose: the three causes (chapter 3) and the after-result decision paragraph (chapter 18) are already short and clear in prose.

Print note: for a 6 x 9 inch page the diagrams scale to about 5 inches wide; the smallest label (22 px in a 1200 px canvas) prints at roughly 6.6 pt. If the owner wants larger print type, drop the "Counting down" line in 05 and the four-box row in 01 into two rows. EPUB readers that do not support SVG need the PNG files (same names).
