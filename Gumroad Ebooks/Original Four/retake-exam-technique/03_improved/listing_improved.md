## Description (paste as plain text)

FAILED A LICENSING EXAM? RE-READING THE SAME BOOKS MAY NOT FIX WHAT WENT WRONG.

A failed result can come from three different places: you did not know the material, you knew it but chose the wrong option, or the exam conditions got to you. Each has a different fix. This book helps you work out which one is costing you the most, then gives you a method for the exam room.

It does not teach your subject and it contains no exam questions. It teaches how to sit a multiple-choice exam, for any profession.

WHO IT IS FOR
• Adults who have failed a professional licensing exam once or more, in any field
• Anyone with a computer-based or paper multiple-choice retake booked or planned
• People who want a method for how to sit the exam, not another content review

WHO SHOULD NOT BUY IT
• Anyone who wants subject content or practice questions
• Anyone looking for a pass guarantee: no book can promise a result
• Candidates whose exam is oral, practical or essay-only

WHAT YOU WILL BE ABLE TO DO
• Read a failed score report word for word and turn it into three clear diagnosis sentences
• Run a two-day diagnostic that separates knowledge gaps, method gaps and nerves
• Spot the qualifier in every question and apply a fixed rule for FIRST, NEXT, BEST and MOST LIKELY
• Break a tie between two plausible answers with an eight-step sequence
• Eliminate options for named reasons, and change an answer only when you can state a fact
• Work out your own time per question, set four checkpoints and use a skip rule
• Use word-for-word scripts for the moments you are stuck, blank or panicking
• Have the retake conversation with your employer and with family, using ready-to-adapt messages
• Keep a practice error log and follow a method-based study week
• Run an exam-day routine and a last-ten-minutes routine
• Plan the next 24 hours, week and month after the result, whichever way it goes

WHAT IS INSIDE
• 20 chapters plus a short about-the-publisher page, about 18,000 words
• Worked examples set in kitchens, bicycles and journeys, so none can be mistaken for exam content
• Chapter by chapter: what a fail does and does not tell you; the three causes; reading your score report; the diagnostic; the anatomy of a question; decision rules; tie-breakers; elimination; pacing; coping scripts; employer and family conversations; error log; study week; exam day; the last ten minutes; after the result
• Five diagrams: the diagnostic, the reading routine, the four decision rules, the tie-breaker sequence and the pacing timeline (in the improved edition)

HOW IT WORKS
Exam-agnostic by design. You take your own exam's length, time limit and rules from your exam body's published information, and the book shows you how to apply each technique to them.

FORMAT
Reflowable EPUB, readable on a phone, tablet or e-reader. No PDF is included at the moment.

IMPORTANT
This is general educational material about exam technique. It does not guarantee any examination result. It is not clinical, legal, tax or financial advice. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by any examination body, licensing authority or professional body.

## FAQs
Q: Does this teach my subject or give practice questions?
A: No. It teaches exam technique only. Use reputable practice material for your exam alongside it; the book explains how.

Q: Will it work for my exam?
A: It is written for multiple-choice, computer-based or paper exams in any profession. It does not cover oral, practical or essay-only exams, and you adapt each technique to your exam's own rules using its published information.

Q: Does it guarantee that I will pass?
A: No. Nobody can honestly guarantee an exam result. The book gives you a method; the result depends on your knowledge, your preparation and the day.

Q: What do I get, and on what device can I read it?
A: One EPUB file, about 18,000 words. It reads on phones, tablets, e-readers and in free reading apps on a computer. There is no printable worksheet pack in the current edition.

Q: Is this connected to my exam body?
A: No. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by any examination body, and it contains no real exam content. All examples are invented.
