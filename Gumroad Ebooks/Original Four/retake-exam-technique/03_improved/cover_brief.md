# Cover brief: The Retake Method

(Read with `02_audit\COVER_CONVENTIONS.md`, which holds three full concepts and the competitor evidence.)

## What the book is
An exam-agnostic technique guide (about 18,000 words, EPUB) for an adult who has failed a multiple-choice professional licensing exam once or more and has a retake booked. It teaches how to read a failed score report, diagnose knowledge vs method vs nerves, apply decision rules to FIRST / NEXT / BEST / MOST LIKELY questions, break ties, pace the clock, talk to employer and family, and run exam day and the last ten minutes. It contains no subject content and no pass guarantee.

## The reader
Working adult, usually 25 to 45, already stressed and a bit embarrassed, price-conscious after paying exam fees, reading on a phone or tablet late in the evening. They are not looking for inspiration; they want to know what to do differently this time.

## Strongest single visual idea
"The score report, annotated." A cream paper score-report sheet (generic, invented, no exam body layout) lying flat, with a few content-area bars, a red "NOT YET" style line at the passing mark (use the words "passing line", not an exam body's terms), and a hand-drawn teal circle and arrow around ONE row with the handwritten note "why?". It says the book's core promise (turn the failed report into a diagnosis) in one image and looks nothing like the stock-photo desk/lightbulb covers. Fallback concept: the four-option answer sheet with two bubbles ringed and a pencil between them (the tie-break moment).

## Title hierarchy
1. Main title (huge, 2 lines max): THE RETAKE METHOD
2. Subtitle (about one third the size, 2 lines): Exam technique for anyone retaking a professional licensing exam
3. One benefit line or badge: "Read your score report. Break the ties. Hold your pace."
4. Author / publisher small at the bottom: The Velvet Foundry (add "Poppy Ebooks" only if the owner wants the imprint visible; the store does not show it)
The live title is far too long for a cover; the long form belongs in the Gumroad title field, not on the image.

## Palette suggestion
Category convention in test prep is light or high-contrast with one hot accent. Suggested: warm off white #f4efe6 base, navy #0f2238 type, ONE accent, copper #b87333 for the annotation marks, teal #3aa6a0 for the "pass" side of the report. Light background so it reads as a workbook, not a thriller. (Keeps the publisher identity without the dark and gold look of the current cover.)

## Avoid
- Any exam body, licence-issuing authority or professional-body name, logo, seal or colour scheme (not affiliated, per the book's own disclaimer).
- Any real exam's score report layout; keep the sheet generic.
- "Pass guaranteed", "100% pass", percentages, stars, awards, "bestseller", testimonial quotes.
- Medical imagery (stethoscopes) or legal gavels: the book is exam-agnostic and its examples are kitchens and bicycles.
- Stock photo of a stressed student; thin light grey type; the current cover's tiny subtitle and empty lower half.
- Yellow or gold as the dominant colour (house rule for extension UIs; keep it out here too).
- Dashes in the file names (house rule); use underscores.
