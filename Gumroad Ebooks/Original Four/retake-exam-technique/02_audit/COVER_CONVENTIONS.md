## What winning-looking covers have in common (observed, not proven to sell)
1. Dominant colours: strong saturated blue (1, 4, 8, 9) or a single warm field (red, orange, beige). Almost none are dark, glossy and gold except the nursing one (9), which pairs navy/gold with badges and proof.
2. Background: more light or mid-tone than dark; the dark navy/gold look is a nursing-exam convention, not a general test-prep one.
3. Title: one enormous 1 to 3 word condensed bold sans, filling a third to half of the image (1, 4, 5, 8, 9). Readable at thumbnail size.
4. The exam or the outcome is named in the title (PMP, FNP, "Second Attempt"). The buyer finds themselves in two seconds.
5. Reader benefit as a short call-out or badge, not a paragraph: "Includes a Complete 60-Day Study Plan" (8), "200 Practice Questions / Instant Download" pills (9), "Based on latest edition of PMBOK" (4).
6. Proof of contents: page mockups and counts (9) are the only cover here that shows what the buyer receives.
7. Imagery: either none (type-only, 1, 5), a single literal photo (2, 8), or a flat illustration (7, 10). Concrete, not abstract.
8. Author/publisher: credentials shown on the one cover with an author (1: "PMP, PMI-RMP"), a brand strip on 9.

## What looks amateur
- Type that disappears: script font over a patterned photo (6), tiny teal subtitle on orange waves (4), the thumbnail cut mid-word (3).
- Mismatch between cover title and product title (2: "Test Taking Success" vs "Test Taking Strategies").
- Clip-art or random stock (2, 7), long bullet lists on the cover (6), more than two fonts.
- Our own current cover: the short title is cut to "RETAKE METHOD: EXAM" (the word THE and the main idea dropped), the title is split across two weights so the head of the phrase reads as the subtitle, the subtitle and author are tiny grey on near-black, the lower half of the image is empty, and the thumbnail is another product's cover.

## Where our book can differ
No competitor seen covers the full retake problem (score report, method, nerves, people). Covers in this niche say "pass" or "strategies" in generic terms. Ours can show the specific tool the book delivers, an annotated score report, and use a short readable title that is still not a pass promise.

## Three cover concepts (designer-ready)

### Concept A: "The Annotated Score Report" (recommended)
- Palette: cream #f4efe6 ground, navy #0f2238 type, copper #b87333 pen marks, teal #3aa6a0 for the area above the passing line.
- Layout (portrait 1600 x 2560 and square thumbnail): top third title, middle two thirds a flat, slightly tilted generic score-report sheet.
- Headline hierarchy: THE RETAKE METHOD (two lines, 14 percent of height each, heavy condensed sans or a bold serif such as Georgia Bold); subtitle in 3 lines at about 4 percent height: "Exam technique for anyone retaking a professional licensing exam"; footer: The Velvet Foundry.
- Image idea: five horizontal bars (content areas), a dotted "passing line", one bar stopped short of it ringed in copper with a hand-drawn arrow and the word "why?". No exam body wording, no real layout.
- Why recognised: buyers of retake guides have looked at exactly this sheet; it signals "I know your situation" without a pass promise.
- Thumbnail: just the title and the ringed bar.

### Concept B: "Two Bubbles" (tie-break moment)
- Palette: white ground, navy type, one electric teal accent #3aa6a0 (high-contrast, workbook feel; comparable to the PMP Success Handbook layout 8).
- Layout: portrait; title in the top 40 percent; below, an answer-sheet grid of four rows of A B C D bubbles where in one row B and C are both half filled and a pencil rests between them.
- Headline: THE RETAKE METHOD / FIRST. NEXT. BEST. MOST LIKELY. as the call-out strip under the title (these are the qualifiers the book teaches, so it is accurate).
- Why recognised: anyone who has sat a multiple-choice exam knows the two-bubble dilemma; the strip names the exact question types candidates complain about.
- Thumbnail: title plus the two ringed bubbles only.

### Concept C: "Badge and Proof" (nursing-style conversion cover)
- Palette: deep blue #0f2238 field with a single teal band and cream badges; avoid gold (house rule) and use copper only on the badge outline. Dark is acceptable here because exam-prep buyers recognise it from category leaders (9).
- Layout: square; top small badge pill "FOR ANY MULTIPLE-CHOICE LICENSING EXAM"; huge title THE RETAKE METHOD; four pill badges: "Score report diagnosis", "8-step tie-breaker", "Pacing plan", "Exam-day routine"; bottom stat row "20 chapters, 5 diagrams" (only if the diagrams edition ships) and publisher strip.
- Image idea: three-up mockup of interior pages (the diagnostic, rules card, pacing timeline) from `03_improved\diagrams`, so the buyer sees what they get.
- Why recognised: copies the proof-of-contents pattern of the busiest exam-prep cover in the sample without claiming questions or a pass.
- Caution: every badge must be true on the day the cover ships; "5 diagrams" is true only for the improved edition.

## Rules for all three
- Short title on the cover (THE RETAKE METHOD); the long form lives in the Gumroad title.
- No pass promise, rating stars, "bestseller", exam body names or logos.
- Test at 150 px wide before approval.
