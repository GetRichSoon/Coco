Text as it appears in the last section of `manuscript_final.md` ("Recommended next steps and further resources"). Only pages actually opened are cited.

**The accounting standard setters,** if you ever want to read the underlying principles: the IFRS Foundation publishes IFRS Accounting Standards, which its website says are required for use by more than 140 jurisdictions. Some countries, the United States among them, use their own national standards instead, so look up the standard setter for your own country. Neither is necessary for a one-person business, and both are freely readable if you are curious about where accounting rules come from.
