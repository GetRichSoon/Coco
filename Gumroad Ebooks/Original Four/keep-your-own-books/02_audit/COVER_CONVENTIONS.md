# Cover conventions for bookkeeping products on Gumroad (read 2026-11-07)

Method: 13 competitor cover images downloaded from each page's JSON into `competitor_covers\` and viewed one by one. One cover per product (the first image). No sales data exists; I describe looks, not performance. All 13 show 0 ratings, so I cannot say which covers "win"; the conventions below are what is common, and what looks like care versus haste.

## What the covers in this niche have in common

- **Light backgrounds.** By my count 11 of 13 are white, cream, pale pink, mint or pale grey. Only the red guide (Accounts Mechanic) and the blue-desk photo (Stressless) are saturated. None is dark navy or black.
- **Product mockup first.** 8 of 13 show a laptop, tablet, phone or a dashboard screenshot. The picture says "tool you can use today", not "reading".
- **Format and delivery badges.** "Google Sheets | Instant Download", "Excel spreadsheet | 5 tabs", a green Sheets/Excel icon, "Reusable every year", "2023 Edition", "Starter / Universal". The buyer is told the file type on the cover.
- **Title is large and literal.** "Easy Bookkeeping", "Small Business Bookkeeping", "The Sole Trader Bookkeeping Guide". The word "bookkeeping" is always legible at thumbnail size. Audience words ("for freelancers", "for small businesses") are in a strip or banner.
- **Benefit line when present is plain:** "Turn client chaos into a smooth ride", "Take control of your finances, tax and track your client payments", "This book will save you hundreds of hours (of agony)".
- **Feature tabs/lists** on the cover ("Instructions, Setup, Income Log, Expense Log, Monthly overview").
- **Warm, friendly palettes** (pink/peach, mint, sunny yellow, soft green) and script or rounded type for the "easy" promise. The one guide that looks like a book (red cover, white bold sans, calculator icon, right-aligned title and clear subtitle) is the closest in category to ours.
- **Seller identity is quiet**: a small "Money Reset UK presents" or "The Accounts Mechanic", never a luxury publisher mark.

## What looks amateur

- Cover that is a company logo, not a product (Excell Bookkeeping Services, $75).
- Generic AI-style icon plus "Bookkeeping made simple" on white (Ledger Lab).
- Garbled screenshot text and an infographic list (the all-in-one Excel template), tiny book thumbnail floating on grey with decorative circles (Creator's Tax Book).
- Dated badges ("2023 Edition") on a page showing a 2026 store.
- Too many callouts, arrows and a yellow strip (ClearLedger) hurts legibility at thumbnail size, but it does communicate "dashboard + tax estimate".

## How our current covers compare

Dark navy, centred serif, cream "KEEP", low-contrast "YOUR OWN", copper squares that mean nothing to a bookkeeper, a subtitle and an author line at roughly 9 to 11 px on a 1005-wide image, a disclaimer at about 7 px. No format badge, no benefit, no device, no signal of "bookkeeping" other than the title. It is a stylish literary cover in a category where buyers scan for a tool and a clear promise. It is distinctive, but the distinction does not help the buyer.

## Rules the designer should follow

1. Light or mid-tone background. Do not go dark/gold/navy; keep Velvet Foundry identity with a small wordmark and a copper rule at the base only.
2. "Bookkeeping" (or a bookkeeping object) must be readable at 300 px wide.
3. Show the benefit in six words or fewer ("Close your month in one hour").
4. Show format (EPUB + PDF) and what is included (if the template pack is added). Never claim a template the buyer does not receive.
5. No exam, tax-authority, software-vendor or bank logos. No "tax advice" claims; "no tax rules" can be a selling line.
6. Required sizes: wide cover 1280 x 720 minimum (live shows 1920 x 1080 native), square thumbnail 600 x 600, plus an in-book cover.

## Three distinct cover concepts

### Concept A: "Ledger page" (book-like, light)

- **Palette:** warm paper cream #f6f1e4, ledger green #1f6f5c, ink #14202b, accent copper #b87333 (hairline rule and small wordmark only).
- **Layout:** portrait book cover; the top two thirds a ruled ledger page with five red-margin lines of handwritten-style entries; the bottom third a solid green band.
- **Headline hierarchy:** KEEP YOUR OWN BOOKS (heavy slab or bold sans, ink on cream, very large) / subtitle "Bookkeeping for freelancers and sole traders" / badge circle "Close a month in 1 hour" / small "The Velvet Foundry" on the green band.
- **Image idea:** a close-up of a two-column ledger with a single tick mark, a pencil, and a bank statement corner peeking out; a hand-drawn circle around "Balance: 5,075 = 5,075" (the book's own worked example).
- **Why a buyer recognises it:** it looks like a ledger book, the object every freelancer has seen in an accountant's office; the badge matches the "month-end in an hour" promise.

### Concept B: "Solid colour guide" (the category's only book-style cover, done properly)

- **Palette:** deep teal #0f6b66 background, cream #f4efe6 type, soft coral #e86f51 accent, copper hairline for the publisher mark.
- **Layout:** full-bleed solid colour, large left-aligned title stack, a white line-art illustration at the right.
- **Headline hierarchy:** KEEP YOUR OWN BOOKS (cream, 2 to 3 lines, very large) / "A simple bookkeeping system for freelancers and sole traders" (coral) / bottom strip "EPUB + PDF | Works in any country | No tax rules".
- **Image idea:** white line drawing of two balance-scale pans labelled "debit" and "credit" with a coin on each, echoing the book's "two halves of every transaction" idea; or a stack of three monthly folders.
- **Why a buyer recognises it:** same family as the red sole-trader guide (solid colour, white bold type, clear subtitle) but warmer and clearer about benefit and format.

### Concept C: "Mockup shelf" (marketplace-style, for the wide poster and thumbnail)

- **Palette:** soft warm grey #efeae1 background, deep green #1f6f5c for the main title, teal #3aa6a0 and coral accents, white device frames.
- **Layout:** 16:9. Left: title block. Right: a tablet and a phone showing real spreads from the book (the "month-end close" diagram and the "profit is not cash" bar chart from `03_improved\diagrams`). Bottom strip: three badges.
- **Headline hierarchy:** KEEP YOUR OWN BOOKS / "Bookkeeping you can actually finish, in one hour a month" / badges "20 short chapters", "EPUB + PDF", "Checklists in every chapter".
- **Image idea:** the book's own diagrams in the device screens (real content, not stock). If the starter template pack is added, add a laptop showing the ledger sheet and a "Free ledger + chart of accounts" badge.
- **Why a buyer recognises it:** matches the dominant marketplace look (device mockup + badges) while showing that this is a readable system, not another spreadsheet.

Recommended: A for the in-book and portrait cover, C for the Gumroad wide cover (first image) and B-style simplification for the square thumbnail (title only, large, three words at most visible: "KEEP YOUR OWN BOOKS" plus "Bookkeeping" word). The designer should test each at 300 px wide.
