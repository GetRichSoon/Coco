# Cover brief: Keep Your Own Books (for the lead designer)

Read with `02_audit\COVER_CONVENTIONS.md` (competitor evidence and three full concepts A, B, C). Owner rule: commercially competitive in its own category, recognisable Velvet Foundry identity, not every book dark/gold/navy.

**What the book is.** A plain-English bookkeeping guide: the double-entry system, a one-hour month-end routine, bank reconciliation, reading profit and loss and balance sheet. About 15,000 words, EPUB (PDF and a starter template pack recommended). No tax rules, works in any country.

**The reader.** A new freelancer or one-person business owner, no accounting background, anxious about "doing it wrong", scanning Gumroad for something that gets the books done. Not an accountant, not a company owner.

**Strongest single visual idea.** A real ledger: a ruled two-column page with a few entries and one ticked line, a pencil, and a hand circle around two equal balances ("5,075 = 5,075", the book's own reconciled June). It says bookkeeping at a glance and shows the promise (it balances, you checked it). Do not use calculators, piggy banks, coins or stock handshake imagery.

**Title hierarchy.**
1. KEEP YOUR OWN BOOKS (largest, 2 to 3 lines)
2. Subtitle: "Bookkeeping for freelancers and sole traders" (must contain the word Bookkeeping at a legible size)
3. Benefit badge: "Close a month in 1 hour"
4. Strip: "EPUB + PDF" (only if a PDF exists) and "No tax rules, works anywhere"
5. Publisher mark "The Velvet Foundry" small with a copper hairline (identity element, same on all Foundry books)

**Palette suggestion.** Light base: paper cream #f6f1e4 or soft warm grey #efeae1; ledger green #1f6f5c or deep teal #0f6b66 for title and bands; ink #14202b; coral #e86f51 sparing accent; copper #b87333 for the publisher hairline only. Avoid navy-dominant and gold. Per the owner's extension rules, avoid marigold/butter yellow.

**Deliverables.** Portrait cover (inside the book), wide cover 1280 x 720 (Gumroad first image, concept C with device mockups showing the book's own diagrams from `03_improved\diagrams`), square thumbnail 600 x 600 (title only plus word Bookkeeping, concept B style).

**Avoid.** Logos or marks of tax authorities, banks, accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero and so on), professional bodies; any "tax advice", "save on tax", "guaranteed", "IRS" or "HMRC" wording; stock photos of people; pink/watercolour "template" look (this is a book); illegible disclaimer text; the meaningless copper squares of the current cover; invented reviews, star ratings or bestseller badges; a template or PDF badge for things not yet included.

**Thumbnail legibility rules.** Test at 300 px wide and at 150 px. Title at least 12 percent of the image height per line at 600 x 600. Contrast ratio at least 7:1 for title, 4.5:1 for everything else. No text under 28 px on the 600 x 600 thumbnail; nothing but title, "Bookkeeping" and the badge. Keep disclaimer text off the thumbnail and out of the wide cover (it stays on the page and inside the book).
