Description (plain text):
You already know ChatGPT can write. The problem is turning that into work you can trust: every new chat starts from nothing, answers come out generic, and you are never sure what to check before you send.

The AI Workflow Playbook gives you 30 repeatable workflows for the jobs a small business does every week, each with a copy-and-paste prompt and a checklist for reviewing the result before anyone sees it. It is a 67-page guide for small business owners, freelancers, solopreneurs, consultants and creators. No technical background is needed.

WHAT YOU GET
• 12 chapters (about 9,300 words)
• 30 workflows, each laid out the same way: problem, outcome, tools, steps, prompt, example input, example output, a verify checklist and common mistakes
• 31 copy-and-paste prompts, plus a reusable prompt template
• 6 worksheets: task audit, business context notes, workflow test log, privacy rules, review ladder and 30-day scorecard
• A one-page AI use policy template you can adapt for your business
• A worked week-1 example and a 30-day implementation plan
• A setup section on ChatGPT projects, memory, file uploads and data controls, described as documented on 7 November 2026
• A glossary and a list of official sources to check

INSIDE THE 30 WORKFLOWS
Foundations: a reusable Business Context Block, a prompt improver, an output-format library.
Research: customer profiles from your own messages, competitor comparison from pages you paste, review theme analysis.
Marketing: a monthly content calendar, one piece in five formats, posts in your voice, blog briefs.
Email: a welcome sequence, customer reply drafting, newsletters from rough notes, a tone check for difficult messages.
Sales: an ideal lead profile, personalized outreach, follow-ups, call prep and summaries.
Customer service: FAQ builder, reply macros, complaint triage.
Admin: meeting notes to actions, proposals, job descriptions.
Data: list cleaning, spreadsheet formulas, plain-English monthly reports.
Systems: an SOP builder, checklists and quick training, and an audit of what to automate first.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT FROM A PROMPT LIST
Every workflow ends with a human check. The book teaches you to anonymize what you paste, mark what the AI does not know, and verify numbers, dates and promises yourself. One rule runs through it: AI drafts, you decide.

WHO IT IS FOR
Small business owners, freelancers, solopreneurs, consultants and creators who already use or want to start using ChatGPT at work.

WHO IT IS NOT FOR
People looking for guaranteed income, "set and forget" automation, or a technical guide to building bots or integrations. The book makes no income or growth promises and does not cover tools such as Zapier.

FAQ
Q: Do I need a paid ChatGPT plan?
A: The workflows are written for a normal chat with pasted text. Some optional features differ by plan, so check what your account offers.

Q: Will it work with other AI assistants?
A: The prompts are plain text and usually adapt, but the book is written for ChatGPT and has not been tested with others.

Q: I do not run a business. Is it useful?
A: Many workflows (email, summaries, SOPs, spreadsheets, meeting notes) suit freelancers and professionals. Chapters 6 and 7 assume you serve customers.

Q: Is it safe to put customer information into ChatGPT?
A: Chapter 11 explains what not to paste, how to anonymize, and which account settings to check. It does not replace advice about the laws and contracts that apply to you.

Q: Does it promise results?
A: No. It gives methods and checklists. Results depend on your business and your own review.
