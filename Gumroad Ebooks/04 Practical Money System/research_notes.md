# Research notes: Book 04, The Practical Money System (2026-11-07)

## Competitor research
- Base: `Organic Research\04_Practical_Money_System_research.md` (22 Gumroad listings with data). Findings used: crowded but low-proof; mostly workbooks, templates and short guides; the closest all-in-one title is a free beginner's guide; "guaranteed/failproof/financial freedom" language is common; Gumroad prohibited-products rules (get-rich, investment opportunities, credit repair, guaranteed outcomes) shape the compliance approach.
- Extra pass: WebSearch for further Gumroad finance listings returned about 20 URLs (e.g. stackedsecure, perryhampton, beginnerbudgetbible, slycoinc, blyxxa, buytopia). **I could not read any of those pages**: WebFetch returned only the page title (Gumroad pages render client-side), so I have NOT read 5 additional competitor pages and cannot report their contents. Only the search-engine summaries are available: range free to about 135 USD, most are short PDFs, some bundle spreadsheets and a net worth calculator, one is 169 pages. These are seller claims, unverified.
- Buyer complaints: one WebSearch for Reddit/forum complaints found no direct threads, only SEO listicles. The only usable themes were secondary (readers like concrete step-by-step instructions; books assuming prior knowledge are too advanced; a debt-averse stance criticised as restrictive). Not verified against Reddit or review pages. Treat the gap analysis below as hypotheses from the research file, not review evidence.

## Gaps and how the book addresses them
| Gap (hypothesis) | Response in the book |
|---|---|
| Few titles combine budget, save, debt and invest | One ordered system across 12 chapters |
| Debt guides give no numbers | Same-debt avalanche, snowball and hybrid comparison, simulated by script, with month by month totals |
| Freelancers poorly served | Lowest-six baseline, pay-yourself-a-salary, holding-account test |
| Generic percent rules | Three worked budgets with arithmetic shown |
| Hype and guarantees | Education only, assumptions labelled, risks and fees quantified, scam chapter |
| International readers | CU currency units; universal principles separated from labelled US/UK examples |
| Worksheets missing or not copyable | 14 copyable worksheets/checklists in text |
