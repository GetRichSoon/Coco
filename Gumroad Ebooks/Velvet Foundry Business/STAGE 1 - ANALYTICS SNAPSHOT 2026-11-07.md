## What this means (and does not mean)
- There is no purchase history to analyse for buyer behaviour, cross-selling, conversion or channel quality. Any such claim would be invented.
- The first problem is not conversion; it is **traffic**: 19 views in about ten days, probably mostly the owner's.
- Three of the four original books are in the Gumroad category "Other", and the Discover banner says a product needs at least one successful sale and the Risk Review before it can appear in Discover. So Discover cannot yet send traffic to any of the nine.
- Launch-date-adjusted rankings between group A and group B are not meaningful yet: every product has zero sales.
- Sample size: 19 views, 0 sales. No conclusion about conversion is statistically possible.
