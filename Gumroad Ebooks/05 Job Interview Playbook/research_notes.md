## Complaints and gaps (limited evidence)
A web search for forum complaints returned archived Reddit snapshots and opinion pieces, not live threads (not verified on Reddit itself). Themes in my own words: memorised answers freeze under follow-up questions; polished "perfect answer" scripts sound fake; prep that only drills a fixed list fails when the question is reworded. Gaps from the earlier file: no graduate or career changer interview title, no STAR frameworks on Gumroad, largest count 55.
How the book addresses them: every question has a framework (not a script), a clearly fictional example, and separate beginner and experienced adaptations; Chapter 6 and 7 teach building answers from true stories; Chapter 10 covers blank minds and follow-ups.

## Differentiation
100 distinct questions in exact category groups; dual beginner/experienced adaptation per question; resume, ATS, LinkedIn, cover letter, salary, follow-up and 30-day planner in one book; honest ATS chapter (no format "beats" ATS); 30-prompt story bank.
