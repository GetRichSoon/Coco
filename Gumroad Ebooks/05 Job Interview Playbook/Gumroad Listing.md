## DESCRIPTION (paste into Gumroad; plain text)
Most people lose interviews on questions they could have prepared for. The Job Interview Playbook gives you 100 common interview questions, and for every one it shows what the interviewer is really trying to learn, a framework for building your own answer, a realistic example, the mistakes to avoid, and how to adapt it whether you are a beginner or experienced.

It is a 97-page guide for students, graduates, entry-level applicants, experienced professionals and career changers. It also covers the rest of the job search: resume, applicant tracking systems, LinkedIn, cover letters, preparation, salary talks and follow-ups.

It does not guarantee interviews or offers, and it does not give you scripts to memorise. Example answers are fictional illustrations; the book teaches you to build every answer from your own true experience.

FAQ
Q: Are the answers ready to copy?
A: No. They are illustrations of structure. Interviewers can tell a rehearsed script, and the book tells you to use your own true experience.

Q: I have no work experience. Is it useful?
A: Yes. Every question has a beginner adaptation, and Chapter 7 shows how to use study, volunteering and projects.

Q: Does it cover technical interviews?
A: It gives a general method for preparing for technical and role-specific questions across fields, with illustrative examples. It does not teach a specific technical subject.

Q: Does it guarantee that I will get a job?
A: No. It helps you prepare and present your experience clearly. Outcomes depend on many things outside your control.

Q: Does it cover salary negotiation?
A: Yes: how to research a range, when to talk about pay, and seven scripts for common situations. Practices differ by country.
