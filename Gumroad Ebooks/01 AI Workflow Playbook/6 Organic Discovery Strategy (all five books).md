# Organic discovery strategy: five-book Gumroad collection

Prepared 2026-11-07. No paid ads, fake reviews, fake sales, misleading discounts or spam anywhere in this plan.

## 1. The honest picture

**Nobody can promise Day-1 sales, bestseller status or first-page placement.** What can be done is to make each page easy to find, easy to understand and honest, so that if people do search, the page converts.

**Discover eligibility (what is and is not known)**
- Official Gumroad help text (article "Gumroad Discover", read through a search summary on 2026-11-07; the article page itself rendered empty in the fetch tool and the search tool flagged it as about 837 days old): a product shows in Discover when a **category is selected** and **ratings are enabled** (Share tab, Discover settings). Hiding ratings removes the product from Discover search.
- Third-party blogs claim additional gates (a minimum sales balance, quoted as $10 by some and $100 by others, plus payout setup and a risk review). **These disagree and are NOT verified.** Check inside your Gumroad dashboard (the products dashboard has a Discover eligibility indicator, per Gumroad's help index).
- Third-party sources also say Discover sales carry a higher fee than direct sales, and that products on Discover are enrolled in the Affiliates program. Check Gumroad's current pricing page before you decide.
- Seller reports on Indie Hackers describe Gumroad's organic discovery as weak for new products. That is anecdotal. Plan for the possibility that early traffic is near zero.

**Status of your five products:** none is published, so none has Discover eligibility today. Each becomes eligible only after it exists with a category and ratings enabled, and then any further requirement Gumroad applies to your account. Whether your account meets those is something only your dashboard can show. Nothing was inspected in your account.

**If organic sales need initial traffic or purchases first:** that is likely, and this plan does not hide it. The only legitimate fix is real customers and real reviews. Do not buy or fake either.

## 3. Title and listing rules for every book

1. Put the buyer's search phrase in the Gumroad title, then the book's brand title: `[Search phrase]: [Brand title] ([N] ...)`.
2. First paragraph: the problem the buyer has, then what they receive, in plain words. No guarantees.
3. Show what is actually inside: the contents list and a **free sample PDF** (for Book 01 it is `The_AI_Workflow_Playbook_FREE_SAMPLE.pdf`, 6 pages: cover, contents, two full workflows).
4. Every claim must match the final PDF. Recount pages, words and templates after any edit.
5. Unique description per book. Do not reuse paragraphs across products (duplicate text weakens search pages).
6. Enable ratings on every product (required for Discover).
7. Use a descriptive custom URL slug and the specific tags listed in each listing file.

## 4. Google organic search

- Gumroad product pages are public web pages. Make the title, first paragraph, headings and summary carry the exact phrases a buyer would type. Do not stuff keywords.
- After publishing, you can use Google Search Console's URL Inspection to request indexing of each product URL (free). Indexing is not guaranteed and placement is not controllable.
- Keep the page honest and specific; thin or copied pages tend not to do well.

## 5. Profile and cross-selling

- A professional Gumroad profile: name, short plain bio (do not invent credentials), a profile image, and the five products arranged as one collection ("Practical Guides").
- Cross-sell pairs (natural, not forced): 01 with 02 (AI for outreach and follow-up; both for freelancers and small businesses); 05 with 04 (career, then money); 03 with 01 (automation, then AI workflows).
- Inside each PDF, add a short "More guides" page only after the other books exist and are published, so no page points to something that is not live.
- Gumroad's own tools for related products and bundles can be used once several products are live. Check the current interface.
- Affiliate opportunities: Discover enrolls products in Gumroad's affiliate program according to third-party sources; confirm and decide whether to leave it on.

## 6. What to do on launch day (no daily promotion)

1. Publish one book at a time only when its PDF, price, cover, thumbnail and description are final and you have approved them.
2. Check the live page on a phone and desktop.
3. Test purchase the product yourself using your own account (refund yourself if needed) to confirm the download works. This is a functional test, not a fake sale; do not use it to inflate numbers.
4. Request indexing in Search Console.
5. Revisit after two to four weeks: look at views, conversion and any questions buyers asked, then improve the page or the PDF (edition 1.1).
