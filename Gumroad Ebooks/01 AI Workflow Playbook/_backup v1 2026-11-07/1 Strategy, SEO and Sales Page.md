## 1. Product strategy

**Product:** a practical implementation guide with 30 copy-and-paste ChatGPT workflows for small-business work.

**Buyer:** small business owners, freelancers, solopreneurs, consultants, creators. They already know ChatGPT exists. They lack repeatable, safe, specific procedures.

**Positioning:** not "what is AI". It is "do this task this way, with this prompt, then check it with this list". Every workflow has the same nine parts so the book works as a reference, not a one-time read.

**Why it can sell:** specific number in the title (30), named audience (small business), named tool (ChatGPT), named outcomes (marketing, sales, daily operations). Vague AI titles are crowded; specific, outcome-led ones are easier to understand at a glance.

**Honest limits to keep in the listing:** no income promises, no "guaranteed growth", no fake reviews. The book teaches a method; results depend on the reader's business.

**Format:** PDF, about 7,000 words as drafted (expand to 8,000+ for the full target), 6 x 9 inch pages, 12 chapters, 30 workflows, prompt library, worksheets, glossary.

**Launch order:** publish Product 01 first, then the other four as a collection. Add a "Bundle" later.

## 3. Gumroad sales description (paste-ready)

### Stop explaining AI. Start using it on Monday morning.

Most AI guides tell you what ChatGPT is. **The AI Workflow Playbook** shows you what to do with it: 30 complete, step-by-step workflows for the jobs a small business really does every week.

Each workflow gives you the problem, the outcome, the exact steps, a **copy-and-paste prompt**, a realistic example, and a **checklist to verify the result** before it reaches a customer.

### What you will be able to do
- Build a reusable "business context" so ChatGPT stops giving generic answers
- Research customers, competitors and reviews from material you already have
- Plan and write a month of marketing content in your own voice
- Draft emails, newsletters and customer replies faster, with human review built in
- Prepare outreach, follow-ups and call briefs for sales
- Turn rough notes into FAQs, SOPs, checklists and meeting actions
- Clean data, write spreadsheet formulas and explain monthly numbers
- Use AI safely: privacy, accuracy and a clear human-review rule
- Follow a 30-day plan that adds one workflow at a time

### What is included
- 12 chapters, about 7,000 words of practical content, PDF
- 30 workflows, each in the same nine-part format
- A reusable prompt library
- 6 implementation worksheets
- 30-day implementation plan
- Glossary and further-learning list

### Who this is for
Small business owners, freelancers, solopreneurs, consultants, creators and professionals who want practical, repeatable ways to use ChatGPT at work. No technical background needed.

### Who this is not for
People looking for get-rich-quick shortcuts. This book does not promise income or growth. It helps you save time and improve consistency. Results depend on your business and on your own review of the output.

### FAQ
**1. Do I need a paid ChatGPT plan?** The workflows are written to work in a standard chat. Some features (file upload, saved instructions) vary by plan and change over time, so check what your account offers.

**2. Will this work with other AI assistants?** The prompts are plain text and usually adapt well to other assistants, but the book is written for ChatGPT-style chat.

**3. Is the content up to date?** The book teaches methods that stay stable and flags features that may change. Check the provider's help pages for the latest interface details.

**4. Can I use the prompts in my business?** Yes. Adapt them freely for your own business. Do not resell the book or the prompt library itself.

**5. Does the book promise results?** No. It gives you processes and checklists. It makes no income or growth promises.
