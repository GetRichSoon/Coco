## 4. Gumroad sales page (paste-ready)

**First paragraph**

You already know ChatGPT can write. The problem is turning that into work you can trust: every new chat starts from nothing, answers come out generic, and you are never sure what to check before you send. *The AI Workflow Playbook* gives you 30 repeatable workflows for the jobs a small business does every week, each with a copy-and-paste prompt and a checklist for reviewing the result before anyone sees it.

### What you get

A 65-page PDF (6 x 9 in) with:
- **12 chapters** covering prompts, market research, marketing content, email, sales follow-up, customer service, admin, spreadsheets, SOPs, safety and a 30-day plan
- **30 workflows**, each laid out the same way: problem, outcome, tools, steps, prompt, example input, example output, a "verify" checklist and common mistakes
- **31 copy-and-paste prompts** plus a reusable prompt template
- **6 worksheets** (task audit, business context notes, workflow test log, privacy rules, review ladder, 30-day scorecard)
- **A one-page AI use policy template** you can adapt
- **A worked week-1 example** and a **30-day implementation plan**
- Glossary and a list of official sources to check

### Inside the 30 workflows
- **Foundations:** a reusable Business Context Block, a prompt improver, an output-format library
- **Research:** customer profiles from your own messages, competitor comparison from pages you paste, review theme analysis
- **Marketing:** a monthly content calendar, one piece in five formats, posts in your voice, blog briefs
- **Email:** a welcome sequence, customer reply drafting, newsletters from rough notes, a tone check for difficult messages
- **Sales:** an ideal lead profile, personalized outreach, follow-ups, call prep and summaries
- **Customer service:** FAQ builder, reply macros, complaint triage
- **Admin:** meeting notes to actions, proposals, job descriptions
- **Data:** list cleaning, spreadsheet formulas, plain-English monthly reports
- **Systems:** an SOP builder, checklists and quick training, and an audit of what to automate first

### Why it is different from a prompt list
Every workflow ends with a human check. The book teaches you to anonymize what you paste, mark what the AI does not know, and verify numbers, dates and promises yourself. One rule runs through it: AI drafts, you decide.

### Who it is for
Small business owners, freelancers, solopreneurs, consultants and creators who already use or want to start using ChatGPT at work. No technical background needed.

### Who it is not for
People looking for guaranteed income, "set and forget" automation, or a technical guide to building bots or integrations. The book makes no income or growth promises and does not cover automation tools such as Zapier.

### Honest notes before you buy
- The workflows run in a normal chat by pasting text. Features such as projects, memory and file uploads are described as documented on 7 November 2026; plans, limits and menus change, so check your own account.
- Examples are fictional and for illustration.
- This is an educational guide, not legal, tax or professional advice.
- The PDF contains no diagrams or images. (Update this line if you add them.)

### FAQ
**Do I need a paid ChatGPT plan?** The workflows are written for a normal chat with pasted text. Some optional features differ by plan, so check what your account offers.

**Will it work with other AI assistants?** The prompts are plain text and usually adapt, but the book is written for ChatGPT and has not been tested with others.

**I do not run a business. Is it useful?** Many workflows (email, summaries, SOPs, spreadsheets, meeting notes) suit freelancers and professionals. Chapters 6 and 7 assume you serve customers.

**Is it safe to put customer information into ChatGPT?** Chapter 11 explains what not to paste, how to anonymize, and which account settings to check. It does not replace advice about the laws and contracts that apply to you.

**Does it promise results?** No. It gives methods and checklists. Results depend on your business and your own review.
